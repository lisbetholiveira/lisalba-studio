# Creative Workflow

## Overview

The Lisalba Studio workflow turns an initial request into a controlled, reviewable creative output:

`Brief -> Creative Direction -> Prompt Builder -> Production -> Review`

Each stage produces a defined artefact and ends with a decision. A project can return to an earlier stage whenever review uncovers a strategic or visual mismatch.

## 1. Brief

### Inputs

- Business or communication objective
- Audience and context of use
- Deliverables, formats and channels
- Brand references and factual source material
- Constraints, exclusions, deadlines and approval owners

### Activities

- Separate confirmed information from assumptions and unknowns
- Clarify the central problem and desired audience response
- Identify privacy, rights, reputational and technical risks
- Define review criteria

### Output

A concise creative brief with open questions and an explicit production boundary.

## 2. Creative Direction

### Activities

- Develop the central concept and narrative
- Define visual codes: palette, typography, composition, imagery, texture and motion
- Establish tone, references and anti-references
- Map the system across required formats

### Output

An approved direction that is specific enough to guide production and flexible enough to support variation.

## 3. Prompt Builder

### Activities

- Convert the direction into modular prompt structures
- Define subject, scene, composition, lighting, lens/camera language, materiality and mood
- Add format requirements and negative constraints
- Document versions and tool-specific adaptations

### Output

Production-ready prompt sets and specifications linked to the approved direction.

## 4. Production

### Activities

- Generate or capture controlled variations
- Curate outputs against the brief rather than novelty alone
- Refine images, layouts, motion or audio as required
- Record relevant tools, versions and transformations

### Output

A small, intentional selection of candidates for formal review.

## 5. Review

### Activities

- Compare the work with the brief and creative direction
- Check craft, consistency, accessibility and technical specifications
- Review factual claims, rights, privacy and AI transparency
- Record corrections and the decision: revise, approve internally or prepare for release

### Output

A documented quality decision. External publication or delivery requires human authorisation.

## Iteration rules

- A strategic mismatch returns to the Brief or Creative Direction stage.
- A visual consistency problem returns to Creative Direction or Prompt Builder.
- A generation artefact or formatting problem returns to Production.
- A factual, rights or transparency issue blocks release until resolved.

## Documentation standard

For each project, retain only what is appropriate for its confidentiality level:

- Brief version and approval state
- Creative direction and review criteria
- Prompt/specification versions suitable for the project record
- Selected outputs and quality notes
- Release decision and required attribution or AI disclosure

Public case studies use sanitised material and never expose credentials, private client information or commercially sensitive working files.
